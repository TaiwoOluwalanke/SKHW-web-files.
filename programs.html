Programs

How It Works

One engagement model, delivered in two sessions, built to reach every girl in the room and stay with her long after we leave.

The engagement model

A two-session journey

We don’t believe a single afternoon changes how a girl sees herself. So each cohort moves through two sessions, spaced weeks apart, that build on each other.

Session 1

Awareness & Foundations

We open with menstrual health education and the distribution of sanitary support materials, so every girl leaves equipped, not just informed. From there, foundational empowerment teaching introduces the language of self-worth, identity, and boundaries that the rest of the program builds on.

  • Menstrual health education
  • Sanitary material distribution
  • Foundational empowerment teaching
Session 2

Reinforcement & Growth

Weeks later, we return. This follow-up session reinforces the lessons on self-worth, confidence, safety, and personal growth — because retention comes from repetition, and real change needs more than one conversation to take root.

  • Confidence & self-worth reinforcement
  • Safety & emotional well-being check-in
  • Personal growth & next steps

Woven through both sessions

The three pillars, in the room

Menstrual Dignity

Hygiene education and materials that keep girls in school and in control of their bodies.

Identity & Empowerment

Mentorship that rewrites the story a girl tells herself about her own worth.

Safety & Emotional Well-being

Consent education, abuse awareness, and real referral pathways when it’s needed.

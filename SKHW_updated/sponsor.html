Ways to Give

Sponsorship isn't only financial.

Fund a Cohort

Cover the cost of materials, mentorship, and facilitation for a set number of girls through both sessions.

Provide In-Kind

Sanitary products, learning materials, transport, or refreshments — practical goods go directly into a session.

Offer a Venue

A safe, private space for sessions — a classroom, hall, or community centre — helps us reach a new group of girls.

Volunteer Expertise

Counsellors, health educators, and mentors who can support facilitation or our referral network.

Corporate Partnership

Multi-cohort partnerships with your CSR or ESG program, including impact reporting for your stakeholders.

Spread the Word

Introduce us to schools, community leaders, or other organisations who'd want to bring a session to their girls.

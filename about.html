Vision & Mission

The change we’re building, one girl at a time.

She Knows Her Worth exists because confidence, safety, and dignity shouldn’t depend on where a girl was born or what her family can afford.

Our Vision

A generation of girls who know their value

We see a future generation of girls who are informed, confident, emotionally supported, and deeply aware of their worth — girls who carry that certainty into every classroom, home, and relationship they enter, regardless of the circumstances they started from.

Our Mission

A holistic model of practical support and mentorship

We combine practical support — starting with menstrual dignity — with education, mentorship, and emotional development. The goal isn’t only to meet a girl’s immediate needs, but to equip her with the knowledge, confidence, and support systems she needs to thrive long after we’ve left the room.

Why this work matters

Beyond the classroom

For many girls, limited access to menstrual hygiene products affects school attendance, concentration, and confidence. Others carry low self-esteem, societal pressure, emotional neglect, or unstable home environments — circumstances that can leave a girl feeling unseen, unsupported, and disconnected from her own potential. We built this initiative to meet all of that, not just one part of it.

The three pillars

How the mission comes to life

Menstrual Dignity

Hygiene, without stigma

Participants receive menstrual health education and sanitary support materials to promote hygiene, reduce period-related stigma, and let every girl manage menstruation with confidence and dignity. This pillar also opens up honest conversation around body literacy, self-care, and reproductive health awareness — in language that’s age-appropriate and never clinical or alarming.

Identity & Empowerment

Knowing her own worth

Through mentorship and guided discussion, girls learn to understand their inherent worth, build healthy self-esteem, and reject limiting narratives imposed by society or circumstance. Sessions centre on confidence-building, personal development, resilience, and boundary-setting — skills she carries far past the program itself.

Safety & Emotional Well-being

Safe spaces, real referrals

We create safe, age-appropriate spaces to educate participants on consent, abuse awareness, personal safety, and emotional health. Girls are empowered to recognise harmful situations, speak up, and seek help when they need it — and where appropriate, we facilitate referrals to trained support systems and counseling resources.

A note from our founder

“At its core, She Knows Her Worth exists to raise a generation of girls who are informed, confident, emotionally supported, and deeply aware of their value. By pairing practical intervention with mentorship and education, we empower girls to rise above limiting circumstances and embrace their full potential with courage and conviction.”

Ogunyipe Oluwadarasimi Ayanfeoluwa
Founder, She Knows Her Worth

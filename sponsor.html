Donate School Supplies

Give a girl the tools she needs to learn with confidence

Donate practical school essentials to secondary-school-aged girls, or make a monetary gift so our team can purchase what a school community needs most.

Learning materials

Study essentials

Help a student arrive prepared for lessons, revision, and assessments.

  • Notebooks
  • Pens, pencils, rulers and erasers
  • Mathematics sets
Most requested School essentials

Everyday confidence

Practical items that help girls participate fully and feel ready for the school day.

  • School bags
  • Socks and school shoes
  • Calculators and water bottles
Dignity & care

Well-being support

Help girls stay comfortable, supported, and ready to learn throughout the term.

  • Sanitary products
  • Personal-care items
  • Educational and well-being resources

Volunteer With Us

Join the team for our upcoming program

We’re looking for volunteers to help deliver our next session. Every volunteer completes a short safeguarding orientation before working with any girls.

Event Host

Welcome girls and guardians, keep the day running smoothly, and support the facilitation team on-site.

Facilitator

Lead or co-lead session content on menstrual dignity, identity, or safety and emotional well-being.

Registration Team

Check in schools and groups, confirm consent paperwork, and keep attendance organized.

Ushering Team

Guide girls and guardians through the venue and help sessions transition smoothly between activities.

Want to help?

Get in touch and tell us which role interests you and when you’re available — we’ll share orientation details and next steps.

Volunteer with us

Safeguarding first. Volunteers and partners never contact participating girls directly. All communication about a girl runs through our team, her school, or her guardian.

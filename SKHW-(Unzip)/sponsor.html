Ways to Partner

Support the work at the level where your organisation can make the greatest difference.

Fund a Cohort

Cover the cost of materials, mentorship, and facilitation for a set number of girls through both sessions.

Provide In-Kind

Supply school essentials, menstrual dignity kits, transport, or refreshments that can be used directly in a programme.

Offer a Venue

A safe, private space for sessions — a classroom, hall, or community centre — helps us reach a new group of girls.

Volunteer Expertise

Counsellors, health educators, and mentors who can support facilitation or our referral network.

Corporate Partnership

Multi-cohort partnerships with your CSR or ESG program, including impact reporting for your stakeholders.

Spread the Word

Introduce us to schools, community leaders, or other organisations that could help us bring a session to more girls.

Sponsor a Program

Give directly toward the specific needs of an upcoming program — materials, transport, refreshments, or venue costs for a session already on our calendar.
